Accessibility Statement

Mindful Horizons is committed to making this website accessible and usable for as many people as possible. We aim to follow modern accessibility best practices and continuously improve based on feedback.

? Target: WCAG 2.2 AA (best effort)

Measures we've taken

  • Keyboard navigation: skip link, visible focus styles, and logical tab order.
  • Semantic structure: headings in order and landmarks where appropriate.
  • Readable design: strong contrast targets, responsive layout, and clear typography.
  • Usable forms: labels, helpful instructions, and clear link text.
  • Media support: alt text for informative images and icons marked decorative when appropriate.
  • Motion awareness: animations reduced where feasible for users who prefer reduced motion.
  • Touch targets: interactive elements sized for easier tapping on mobile (aiming for 44px+).

Known limitations

This is a school-project website and may contain accessibility gaps. If you encounter an issue, please let us know so we can improve it.

  • Some third-party icons or embedded content may have limited accessibility controls.
  • Color contrast may vary slightly across devices and displays.

Feedback and contact

If you experience difficulty using this site, please reach out through our Contact page and include:

  • The page you were on
  • What you were trying to do
  • Your device/browser (if you know it)
  • Any assistive technology you use (optional)

Testing approach

We review pages using keyboard-only navigation and basic screen-reader checks when possible, and we test across common screen sizes (mobile, tablet, desktop). Accessibility is treated as an ongoing process, not a one-time task.
